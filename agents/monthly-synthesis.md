# monthly-synthesis

**Schedule**: Every Sunday 20:00 IST (gate-checks if last Sunday of month)  
**Task ID**: `monthly-synthesis`  
**MCPs**: notion, whoop

## Purpose

Gate: runs every Sunday but skips unless today is the last Sunday of the month.

On last Sunday: reads weekly_lessons.md for the month + Notion sessions + Whoop trends, drafts a 600-word synthesis essay in Param's voice (Kerouac/Bukowski energy, no fluff), saves to `essays/YYYY-MM-synthesis.md`, opens a PR.
